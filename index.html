In defence of the familiar

Tourism has a way of turning a city into a list of approved accomplishments. One is expected to admire the monuments, take the photograph, and move along. I grew up in Hamburg and have spent many years in California; on returning, I find this programme singularly unappealing. Give me the Elbe in shit weather, a street with some age to it, a Fischbrötchen that requires both hands, and a Kneipe where nobody considers a stranger's arrival an event.

This map is an argument against the tyranny of the must-see. It includes old restaurants, Barmbek bars, cocktail rooms, secondhand bookshops and fragments of a city's history—some remembered, others still to be investigated. I make no claim that these are Hamburg's finest establishments. The point is to find out for myself, preferably without a queue or an influencer explaining what to feel.
