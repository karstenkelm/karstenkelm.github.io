A few things I miss about Hamburg

I’m originally from Hamburg, but I’ve been living in California for a long time. When I come back, I don’t really need to tick off the big sights. I’d rather walk along the Elbe, look at the old streets, stop for a Fischbrötchen and spend an evening in a proper neighborhood Kneipe.

I’ve put together this map partly to remember the old places and partly to find a few new ones. There are traditional restaurants, Barmbek pubs, cocktail bars, bookshops and bits of Hamburg history. It’s a personal list, not a ranking—and I expect I’ll keep adding to it.
